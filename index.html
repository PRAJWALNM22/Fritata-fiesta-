Our Brand Philosophy

Redefining the Egg Experience in Bengaluru

Frittata Fiesta Café (Egg AddA) was born out of an unapologetic obsession with the humble egg. We believe the egg is the world's most versatile, nutritious, and delicious culinary canvas.

Whether you crave the rustic elegance of slow-baked Italian Frittatas, the fiery nostalgic aroma of Surat Egg Ghotala, or a 40g+ protein workout refuel, our live kitchen crafts every preparation fresh, using graded farm eggs and pure ingredients.
